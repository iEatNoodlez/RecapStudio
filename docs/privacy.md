# Privacy policy

Recap Studio is a program that runs on your own computer. This page says what it does with your information, and it's short because the answer is mostly nothing.

**Nothing is collected by Recap Studio's makers.** The program has no account, no sign-up and no server of its own. It doesn't send usage statistics, crash reports or anything else about you anywhere. Your projects, settings and keys stay in its data folder on your computer (or on your own server, if you run it as one).

**Services you choose to use.** Some features talk to other companies' services, each only when you turn it on and with your own account or key: a script writer such as Claude, Gemini or another online model; a voice such as Microsoft Edge's or ElevenLabs; an image model for the thumbnail; sites you add as sources for chapters; and, with the posting plugins, YouTube and TikTok. What you send them (your chapters' text and pictures, the narration, the finished video) is covered by that service's own privacy policy, not this one.

**Posting to TikTok or YouTube.** The Post to TikTok and Post to YouTube plugins sign in to your account through a developer app you create yourself on that platform. The sign-in tokens they're given are stored only on your computer, in the program's data folder, and are used only to post the videos you choose. **Disconnect** in the plugin's settings gives the access back, and you can also remove the app from your account on the platform itself. The plugins post nothing without your say-so: either you press Post, or you've set a project to post its video when it's made.

**Your phone and the server edition.** The phone page and Recap Studio Server are reached over your own network, behind a login you set. They're not reachable from the internet unless you make them so.

**Updates.** On start, the program asks GitHub whether a newer release exists. That request carries no information about you beyond what any download does.

**Questions.** Open an issue at [github.com/iEatNoodlez/RecapStudio](https://github.com/iEatNoodlez/RecapStudio/issues).
