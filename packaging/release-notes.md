**Which file?** Recap Studio comes in two editions, both free. They keep the same projects and settings, and installing one over the other switches between them. Each copy updates to its own edition.

- **Recap Studio Lite**: `Recap-Studio-Lite-{version}-setup.exe` (installs it) or `Recap-Studio-Lite-{version}-portable.zip`. The whole app, with no plugins. You can add single plugins in Settings → Plugins.
- **Recap Studio Full Suite**: `Recap-Studio-Full-Setup-{version}.exe` or `Recap-Studio-Full-Portable-{version}.zip`. Lite with [Recap Studio's plugins](https://github.com/iEatNoodlez/RecapStudio-Plugins#readme) built in: finding the series, what's trending to make a project of, automation, posting to YouTube, branding, passing a site's Cloudflare check yourself in a window, more languages and file types.

Copies on 0.1.x update to Lite. To move to Full Suite, install its setup over Lite once.

## What's new

- **It opens straight away:** the window appears as soon as you start Recap Studio, saying it's starting, and the app is ready about 4 seconds sooner than before. If it can't start, the window says so and where to find out why.
- **Search results as they're found:** **Get chapters from a site → All your sources** shows each site's results as soon as it answers, so you can pick one while slower sites are still being asked. Searches no longer wait on a Suwayomi server that isn't running.
- **Characters from AniList for a series from your sources** (Find the series 1.2.0, in Full Suite and from Settings → Plugins): pick a series from one of your source plugins, for its chapters, and **Get characters from AniList** (or Kitsu) finds the same series there and gets its characters. Find the series' results also show as each source answers.
